Follow Your DollarThe solution

A public trail for every hop of a donation, with a receipt behind each one.

Donors

Watch where it goes

Give to a campaign and see each payment as a hop on a live map. Check any hop yourself, no trust required.

NGOs

Prove it, don't claim it

Attach a receipt to every payment. A matching receipt releases the funds on its own.

The money stays in escrow until the receipt matches what was declared.

Follow Your DollarHow it works

Escrow, read, decide, release.

1

Donate

Donors fund an NGO campaign.

2

Pay onward

The NGO pays a vendor and uploads the receipt. Funds go into escrow.

3

AI reads

Claude vision proposes the receipt total.

4

Contract decides

Is it within 1% of the declared spend? That rule is on-chain.

Match

The contract pays the recipient. The hop turns green on the map.

Mismatch

Funds stay held. A reviewer approves, or sends the money back to the campaign.

Follow Your Dollar04 / 11
Follow Your DollarWhy Web3, why AI

AI proposes. Code and the chain prove.

The model

Reads.

Pulls the total off a messy receipt as schema-checked JSON. It never decides validity.

The contract

Decides.

The match rule is plain Solidity that anyone can read. No model, no back room.

The chain

Remembers.

Hops, receipt hashes and results are public. Even the NGO cannot edit them later.

Without Web3 you trust the NGO's database. With it, you check the trail yourself.
